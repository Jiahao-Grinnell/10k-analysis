INTANGIBLE INVESTMENT — categories (verbatim from Intangible_investment.docx)

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Research & Development (R&D) | Creates new products, technologies, and production processes; improvement in technologies, design, and production processes; |
| Advertising, Marketing-related intangibles | Advertising campaigns, market research, brand development, trademarks to build customer recognition and demand, increase sales |
| Selling, General & Administrative (SG&A) (i.e. Management & organizational capital) | Management systems, business-process improvements, Improves how firms organize production, Improves production, management, data processing, employee training programs, and organizational capabilities to build workers' human capital and firm-specific skills |
| Intellectual property | Patents, copyrights, trademarks, licenses; Creates/controls economically valuable knowledge and rights |
